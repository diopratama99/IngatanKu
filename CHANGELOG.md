## [1.0.0-beta] - 2026-10-29

The first beta, for Android.

### Added

- **Save from any app.** Share a link from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X or a browser straight to IngatanKu, without copying it.
- **Bolubi writes the note.** The title, a summary and 2 to 4 tags are made for you, and you can check and change them before saving.
- **Library** for everything you save, with search and tags.
- **Your own notes** with photos, headings and lists.
- **Ask Bolubi.** The assistant answers from your own notes, shows the notes it used, and remembers the conversation.
- **Daily quiz.** Three questions a day from your notes become Bolubi's meals, plus a weekly feast.
- **Bolubi grows** from an egg all the way to Sage. You can name it and dress it up.
- **Home screen widget** that shows whether Bolubi has eaten today.
- A daily reminder so you don't forget to feed Bolubi.
- Sign in with Google or with email.
- English and Indonesian.
- Notes still open when you're offline.

### Beta limitations

- Android only.
- Link write-ups and questions to the assistant have a daily limit per account.
- Deleting your account isn't in the app yet; for now, ask through the contact on the About page.
